Frequently asked questions

Questions before you begin.

How do I get a quote?

Send your business type, required pages, features and budget through the enquiry form. We can discuss the scope before confirming a quote.

Are the displayed prices final?

No. They are indicative starting prices. The final amount depends on agreed scope, content, integrations and third-party costs.

Is domain and hosting included?

Not automatically. Domain, hosting, paid tools and other third-party costs are discussed separately before purchase.

Can I request changes?

Yes. The expected number and type of revisions should be agreed before work begins.

Will the website work on mobile?

The design prioritises responsive layouts. Pages should be checked on common screen sizes before launch.

Can you build a full online store?

We can discuss requirements. Secure payments, inventory, user accounts and backend functions need separate planning and may cost extra.
